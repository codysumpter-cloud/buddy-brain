# Independent Norn parity scoring

Buddy Brain scores `prismtek-norn-parity-arena-v1` receipts generated by the cortex-off Prismtek Buddy Core runtime.

The game is not allowed to establish parity merely by setting `passed: true`. The scorer recomputes every scenario hash and the complete receipt hash, ignores runtime pass flags while judging measurements, and applies thresholds owned outside the game repository.

## Scored behaviors

| Scenario | Independent requirement | Weight |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Object learning and reversal | The initially rewarded object is selected; after reversal the alternative is selected, the old value is strongly negative, and the replacement is strongly positive | 20 |
| Unseen-category transfer | Food-category learning exceeds `0.25`, the novel object has zero exact history, and the novel food is selected | 15 |
| Multi-step need planning | Exactly three unique food/rest/play steps relieve all three drives and every step preserves host validation | 15 |
| Relationship-scoped social teaching | Teaching is rejected without contact, transfer stays within the `0.85` cap, the learner gains a bounded association, and learns the grounded word | 15 |
| Ecology-driven survival | Living ecology produces food, hunger selects the namespaced living species, and confirmed harvest yields meaningful nutrition | 15 |
| Hereditary lineage | Conception produces exactly one named generation-one child with two parents | 10 |
| Learning and plan persistence | Restore succeeds, the plan is identical, and category learning survives with no measurable drift | 10 |

All seven independent judgments and the runtime summary must agree for a green `100/100` result. A score from 70 to 99 is yellow but does not establish the v1 boundary. Below 70 is red.

## Trust boundary

The scorer rejects:

- unsupported schemas;
- cortex-on receipts;
- missing, duplicate, or unknown scenarios;
- missing measurements;
- modified scenario or receipt payloads whose SHA-256 no longer matches;
- weak behavior hidden behind runtime `passed` flags;
- social transfer that exceeds the runtime's declared bound.

The scorer itself grants no permissions and performs no game actions. It evaluates evidence only.

## Claim boundary

A green score establishes the seven deterministic, cortex-off, Norn-style artificial-life behaviors for the exact tested receipt. It does not establish consciousness, subjective feeling, unrestricted general intelligence, OpenC2E equivalence, legacy Creatures compatibility, or performance in scenarios that were not tested.

Broader parity requires a runnable OpenC2E comparison adapter, stochastic and long-duration population trials, cross-room and cross-game transfer, multi-generation behavioral studies, and human-visible in-game playtests.
